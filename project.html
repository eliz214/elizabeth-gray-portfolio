Background

After launching Lorem Mail, a platform that helped users send messages to loved ones, we set out to build Lorem Connect — a mobile and web app for messaging, voice, and video calls. Navigating strict regulations, I led user research and design efforts to make the experience feel more human. I collaborated closely with engineers and our principal product designer to create an interface that was both accessible and modern.

Lorem Ipsum Feature One

Problem

Calls are one-way, which means only one party can initiate them. But busy users juggling work and caregiving often miss these calls. Due to some review delays, not all users can respond in time to coordinate a callback, so real-world connection becomes nearly impossible in those fleeting moments.

Solution

I designed, tested, and advocated for a feature that allows users to send a quick, approved reply immediately after a missed call, bypassing the standard review delay. Qualitative feedback showed that it reduced the frustration of missed calls significantly.

Lorem Ipsum Feature Two

Problem

For-profit platforms have historically prioritized operational needs over the user experience. In practice that means users are often left in the dark about whether a message was delivered or why a call dropped, adding emotional strain to an already fragile lifeline.

Solution

I designed and tested clear message statuses, call timer alerts, and respectful language to help people better understand the system and focus on what matters most: staying connected.

Lorem Ipsum Feature Three

Problem

A key challenge given platform constraints was maintaining trust as a good-faith service. Our user base is naturally skeptical of both hyper-vigilant systems and profit-driven third-party services, requiring us to continuously emphasize that the platform is for people, not for profit.

Solution

After identifying key moments in the experience where oversight occurs, I designed unobtrusive cues to indicate who conducts that oversight. This transparency, paired with a clean and modern interface, helped reduce perceived surveillance.

Impact

Lorem Connect has become a core tool for users navigating this space, supporting over 150,000 people across several regions. Since launch, the platform has facilitated more than 3 million minutes of free calls and video chats, along with hundreds of thousands of messages and requests. By eliminating per-minute fees, the platform has saved users an estimated $2.5 million in costs to date.
